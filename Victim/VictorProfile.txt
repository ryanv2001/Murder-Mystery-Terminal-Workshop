SUBJECT IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full Name:           Victor von Jacobson
Age:                 58
Occupation:          Venture Capitalist / Private Estate Trustee
Residence:           Jacobson Manor, 1000 Crestview Ridge
Status:              DECEASED

BACKGROUND & REPUTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Victor von Jacobson made his fortune through leveraged buyouts, high-risk offshore
investment syndicates, and estate foreclosures. Known across financial circles as a
ruthless litigator, he routinely utilized private investigations, forensic debt
audits, and aggressive contract clauses to eliminate business partners and extract
wealth from vulnerable associates.

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Scheduled an exclusive, closed-door dinner at Jacobson Manor on October 31.
• Invited four specific individuals: his estranged sister (Evelyn), his private
  wealth advisor (James), an antique dealer partner (Jaque), and his corporate
  accountant (Christiana).
• Commissioned an independent forensic audit of his offshore shell holdings and
  personal safety deposit vaults 48 hours prior to the dinner.
• Threat assessments indicated Victor intended to confront attendees regarding
  outstanding liabilities, fraudulent transfers, and regulatory tip-offs.

INVESTIGATIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Victor von Jacobson was found dead on the manor grounds near the balcony perimeter.
His private digital terminal, located in the attic server vault, was found locked
under multi-layer encryption.
